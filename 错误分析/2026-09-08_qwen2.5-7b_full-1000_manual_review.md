## 主要错误模式与代表性示例

### 模式一：检索未命中 / "上下文未提"式回避（数量最多，后三册尤甚）

模型未检索到答案时直接输出"上下文没有提到 / 未说明 / 无信息"，而答案是书中的明确事实。

- [8] Q: On what holiday were Harry's parents killed? ｜参考: Halloween ｜答: *"It is not specified in the given context … The context does not contain this information."*
- [10] Q: Who brought baby Harry to the Dursleys' doorstep? ｜参考: Rubeus Hagrid ｜答: *"The context does not provide information …"*
- [16] Q: Where does Harry sleep at the Dursleys' house? ｜参考: The cupboard under the stairs ｜答: *"The text does not specify …"*
- [22] Q: Where do the Dursleys hide from the letters? ｜参考: A hut on a rock in the sea ｜答: *"The context does not mention the Dursleys hiding from any letters."*

### 模式二：人物/实体张冠李戴或跨册混淆

答错人或把后几册/别处情节的人物搬到本题。

- [61] Q: Who is the Hogwarts caretaker? ｜参考: Argus Filch ｜答: Rubeus Hagrid（book1/2/3 同题均答错）
- [69] Q: Who is the Gryffindor Quidditch captain? ｜参考: Oliver Wood ｜答: Angelina Johnson（她后期才任队长）
- [78] Q: Who are Gryffindor's two Beaters? ｜参考: Fred and George Weasley ｜答: Jimmy Peakes and Ritchie Coote（后几册人物）
- [89] Q: Who did the trio wrongly suspect of trying to steal the Stone? ｜参考: Professor Snape ｜答: Quirrell（把"真正的坏人"当成了"起初被怀疑对象"）
- [172] Q: Who is the caretaker of Hogwarts? ｜参考: Argus Filch ｜答: Rubeus Hagrid

### 模式三：答非所问 / 复述检索文本而不给答案

模型把命中的 passage 原样倒出，却没有落到问题要求的实体、时间或原因上。

- [21] Q: When is Harry's birthday? ｜参考: July the thirty-first ｜答: 大段花园/树篱引文，未给出日期
- [26] Q: How do you open the wall behind the Leaky Cauldron? ｜参考: Tap the brick three up and two across ｜答: *"Open," said in a low, faint hiss*（串到第二部密室开门场景）
- [36] Q: What were Dumbledore's four words to open the start-of-term feast? ｜参考: Nitwit, blubber, oddment, tweak ｜答: Expecto Patronum
- [104] Q: Who was drinking the unicorn blood? ｜参考: Voldemort through Quirrell ｜答: 只描述"A hooded figure was drinking the unicorn's blood"，未点名

### 模式四：数字/时间/数量近似错误

- [17] Q: How many birthday presents did Dudley count? ｜参考: Thirty-six ｜答: 37
- [41] Q: How many Weasley children are there? ｜参考: Seven ｜答: Nine
- [143] Q: How old does Harry turn at the start of the book? ｜参考: Twelve ｜答: 17
- [237] Q: How long does the Polyjuice Potion take to brew? ｜参考: About a month ｜答: An hour

### 模式五：机制/情节因果答错或编造细节

- [35] Q: What do Harry's and Voldemort's wands have in common? ｜参考: A phoenix-feather core from the same phoenix ｜答: Both wands are made of holly（材质答错）
- [183] Q: What message appears on the wall after Mrs. Norris is attacked? ｜参考: The Chamber of Secrets has been opened. Enemies of the heir, beware. ｜答: "THE RIDDLE IS AWAKENING"
- [218] Q: What does Harry pull from the Sorting Hat to fight the Basilisk? ｜参考: The Sword of Gryffindor ｜答: 描述 Neville 拔剑（第七部情节）
- [274] Q: How are the petrified victims revived at the end? ｜参考: Mandrake Restorative Draught ｜答: 串到第四部"骨/肉/血"复活仪式
- [309] Q: What is James Potter's Marauder nickname? ｜参考: Prongs ｜答: Moony（绰号互换，Moony 是 Lupin）

## 小结

- 错误首先由**检索召回**决定：答案未进入上下文时，模型要么回避（模式一），要么从错误上下文复制实体（模式二/三）。
- 模型"有上下文但选错事实"与"无上下文直接编造/串联跨册情节"两类错误同样常见（模式二/三/五）。
- 后三册（book6/book7）细节题密集、跨册同名人物与事件多，正确率明显低于前三册；book6 37.1%、book7 34.5% 为最低两档。
